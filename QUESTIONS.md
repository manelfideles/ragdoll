**Q**: For a 15-page paper, the LLM can read the whole thing. Why bother with retrieval?” 
**A**: Fair point on that one document. Often, frontier models score near-perfectly retrieving a single verbatim sentence from a 1M-token context. Enterprise questions aggregate (“every contract whose deductible exceeds €5,000”), compare (“clause 12 across these three policies”), or summarize across many passages. None of those is a single verbatim sentence to find. Plus, having defaulting to retrieval is what makes the pipeline survive both moves: from one short paper to one long contract, and from one document to a whole corpus.
